# T_ALERT_THRESHOLDS — Alert and information thresholds

Source: Directive (EU) 2024/2881, Annex I – Section 4  
Used by: M_LIMITS, future modules `M_PUBLIC_INFO`, `M_EPISODES`

---

## Section A — Alert thresholds

Alert thresholds trigger urgent human health protection measures.

### Measurement notes (regulatory)

- SO₂ and NO₂: hourly mean over **three consecutive hours**
- PM10 and PM2.5: daily mean over **three consecutive days or fewer**
- O₃: hourly mean; for Art. 20 the exceedance is measured or forecast
  for **three consecutive hours**
- Measurements are taken at sites representative of an area ≥ 100 km²
  or of the entire zone if smaller

---

### Table A — Alert thresholds

| Pollutant | Averaging period | Alert threshold |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|
| SO₂ | 1 hour | 350 µg/m³ |
| NO₂ | 1 hour | 200 µg/m³ |
| PM2.5 | 1 day | 50 µg/m³ |
| PM10 | 1 day | 90 µg/m³ |
| O₃ | 1 hour | 240 µg/m³ |

---

## Section B — Information thresholds

Information thresholds activate obligations to inform the public.

### Measurement notes (regulatory)

- SO₂ and NO₂: hourly mean
- PM10 and PM2.5: daily mean
- O₃: hourly mean
- Sites representative of an area ≥ 100 km² or of the entire zone

---

### Table B — Information thresholds

| Pollutant | Averaging period | Information threshold |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------------|
| SO₂ | 1 hour | 275 µg/m³ |
| NO₂ | 1 hour | 150 µg/m³ |
| PM2.5 | 1 day | 50 µg/m³ |
| PM10 | 1 day | 90 µg/m³ |
| O₃ | 1 hour | 180 µg/m³ |

---

## Notes

- Alert and information thresholds **are not limit values**
  and **are not used for compliance assessment**.
- Thresholds are used for:
  - public communication
  - acute episode management
  - activation of temporary measures
