# T_ASSESS_THRESHOLDS — Assessment thresholds

Source: Directive (EU) 2024/2881, Annex II  
Used by: M_ASSESS, M_NETWORK

---

## Section 1 — Human health protection

| Pollutant | Averaging period | Assessment threshold | Unit |
|-----------|------------------|----------------------|------|
| PM2.5 | Calendar year | 5 | µg/m³ |
| PM10 | Calendar year | 15 | µg/m³ |
| NO₂ | Calendar year | 10 | µg/m³ |
| SO₂ | 24-hour mean | 40 | µg/m³ |
| Benzene | Calendar year | 1.7 | µg/m³ |
| CO | 24-hour mean | 4 | mg/m³ |
| Pb | Calendar year | 0.25 | µg/m³ |
| As | Calendar year | 3.0 | ng/m³ |

---

## Notes

- Thresholds expressed as 24-hour mean or maximum 8-hour mean
  are evaluated as the **99th percentile**, equivalent to **3 exceedance days per year**.
- Assessment thresholds:
  - are not limit values,
  - are not used for compliance,
  - serve only to determine the assessment regime.
