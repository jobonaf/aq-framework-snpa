## Section 2 — Ozone (O₃)

### A. Definitions

**AOT40**: cumulative ozone exposure above 80 µg/m³,
calculated as the sum of hourly differences between concentrations above 80 µg/m³ and 80 µg/m³,
considering only hours from 08:00 to 20:00 CET.

---

### B. Ozone target values

| Purpose | Averaging period | Target value | Condition |
|---------|------------------|--------------|-----------|
| Human health protection | Maximum daily 8-hour mean | 120 µg/m³ | not to be exceeded more than 18 times per calendar year as a 3-year mean |
| Vegetation protection | May–July | AOT40 = 18 000 µg/m³·h | 5-year mean |

---

### C. Long-term ozone objectives (by 1 January 2050)

| Purpose | Averaging period | Objective |
|---------|------------------|-----------|
| Human health protection | Maximum daily 8-hour mean | 100 µg/m³ (99th percentile, max 3 days/year) |
| Vegetation protection | May–July | AOT40 = 6 000 µg/m³·h |

## Notes

- The values are transcribed from Annex I of Directive (EU) 2024/2881.
- Exceedance conditions (maximum number of allowed exceedances) are integral
  to the definition of a limit value.
- Limit values, target values and long-term objectives **are not interchangeable**
  and must be treated separately in assessment modules.
