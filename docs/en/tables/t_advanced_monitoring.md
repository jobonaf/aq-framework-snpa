# T_ADVANCED_MONITORING - Advanced monitoring obligations (Art. 9–10)

Strengthened monitoring obligations in critical contexts.

| Condition | Action | Reference |
|-----------------------------------|------------------------------|------------------|
| Elevated persistent concentrations | install supersites | Art. 9(3) |
| Complex emission contexts (traffic, industry) | upgrade network | Art. 10 |
| High population exposure | increase measurement density | Art. 9, 10 |

---

## Notes

- these obligations do not require quantitative formulas
- they can result from combinations of factors
- future extensions: `M_SUPERSITE`, `M_ADVANCED_MONITORING`
