# T_EXPOSURE_OBLIGATIONS — Average exposure reduction

Source: Directive (EU) 2024/2881, Annex I – Section 5  
Used by: M_EXPOSURE (partially), future modules `M_ROADMAP`

---

## Section A — Average exposure indicator (AEI)

| Element | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Definition | Annual average concentration over three calendar years |
| Stations | Urban background sites |
| Scope | Average exposure units |
| Calculation | Average of the current year and the two preceding years |
| Exclusions | Contributions from natural sources, if documented |

---

## Section B — Average exposure reduction obligations (from 2030)

### PM2.5

| AEI 10 years earlier | Reduction obligation |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| < 10.0 µg/m³ | −10% or ≤ 8.5 µg/m³ (whichever is lower) |
| ≥ 10.0 and < 12.0 µg/m³ | −15% or ≤ 9.0 µg/m³ |
| ≥ 12.0 µg/m³ | −25% |

---

### NO₂

| AEI 10 years earlier | Reduction obligation |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| < 20.0 µg/m³ | −15% or ≤ 15.0 µg/m³ (whichever is lower) |
| ≥ 20.0 µg/m³ | −25% |

---

### Transitional note

For the 2030–2032 calculations, Member States may exclude the year 2020.

---

## Section C — Average exposure concentration objectives

| Pollutant | AEI objective |
|-----------|----------------|
| PM2.5 | 5 µg/m³ |
| NO₂ | 10 µg/m³ |

---

## Notes

- Reduction obligations **are distinct** from limit values.
- Achieving the AEI objective may make some percentage obligations inapplicable.
- These elements are candidates for a future `M_ROADMAP` module.
